## Maintenance Notes

- Keep one entry per paper.
- Do not upload publisher PDFs unless redistribution is explicitly allowed.
- Prefer DOI, IEEE, Springer, ACM, ScienceDirect, or official project links.
- Add dataset/code links only when they are public or approved for sharing.
- When a paper belongs to multiple topics, list it in the most relevant topic and add a secondary topic only when it helps readers find the work.
